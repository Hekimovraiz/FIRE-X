About the Project

Fire Safety Science in Orbit

FIRE-X aggregates and analyzes NASA's most comprehensive microgravity combustion database. Without gravity, fire behaves fundamentally differently — flames become spherical, cool flames persist, and conventional fire safety wisdom no longer applies.

From FLEX droplet flames to SAFFIRE large-scale spacecraft burn tests — all canonical NASA PSI data.
