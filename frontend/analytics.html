NASA GLENN RESEARCH CENTER // FINDINGS ARCHIVE

Three Fundamental Microgravity Combustion Laws

LAW 01 // DIFFUSION DOMINANCE

Absence of Natural Convection

On Earth, buoyancy lifts hot gases upward, pulling fresh oxygen inward. In 10⁻⁴ g microgravity, oxygen transport is purely molecular diffusion. Flames become perfectly spherical and burn at lower flame temperatures (1400–1650 K vs 2200 K on Earth).

LAW 02 // COOL FLAME PERSISTENCE

Low-Temperature Chemistry

NASA FLEX experiments revealed that after visible hot flames extinguish, droplets continue burning invisibly via cool-flame chemical kinetics at 600–800 K. Standard spacecraft fire detectors fail to sense this stealth regime.

LAW 03 // VENTILATION FLAMMABILITY

Forced Convection Paradox

Extinguishing a fire by shutting down cabin ventilation can actually worsen flammability for small airflow regimes (2–6 cm/s), where convective oxygen feed maximizes burning rate before radiative losses can quench the reaction.
