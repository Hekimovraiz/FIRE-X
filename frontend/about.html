Project Overview

The Science Behind FIRE-X

Why Microgravity?

In microgravity, buoyancy-driven convection is absent. Flames become spherical. Cool flames persist. Standard fire safety models fail. FIRE-X analyzes NASA's most comprehensive microgravity combustion database to develop new safety models for space habitats.

The Dataset

879 canonical experiments across 24 NASA flight & research families: FLEX-1/2 (droplet combustion), BASS-I/II (solid materials), SAFFIRE I-VI (large-scale spacecraft burns), ACME/CIR, SOFIE, SLICE, and NASA-STD-6001 material testing — all from NASA's Physical Science Informatics (PSI) repository.

AI Integration

The platform uses OpenAI GPT-6-luna via the Responses API with a specialized NASA combustion system prompt. All AI calls are server-side only — the API key is never exposed to the browser. A deterministic SQL+RAG fallback ensures answers are always grounded in real experimental data.

Mission Simulator

The Fire Hazard Index (FHI) formula is derived from NASA-STD-6001 oxygen partial pressure and mole fraction sensitivity curves: FHI = 1 + (O₂/21) × 3.5 + (pO₂/21.3) × 1.8. Validated against ISS Standard (FHI≈5.1), Artemis Exploration Atmosphere (FHI≈8.3), and Hypoxic Haven (FHI≈2.1) profiles.
